# Support the project

[简体中文](../sponsor.md) | English

TerraView is free, open-source software under the Apache-2.0 license, built and maintained by an individual maintainer in their spare time. If it has helped your project, please consider sponsoring it.

## Where the money goes

- **New features** on the roadmap: vector tiles (MVT), direct IFC loading, an IoT data pipeline, measurement and section tools, and more.
- **Testing on real hardware**: checking image quality and performance on integrated, discrete and mobile GPUs, which the software renderer in CI cannot cover.
- **Documentation and examples**: keeping the user manual, the runnable examples and the English documentation up to date.
- **Data and services**: hosting and licensing the maps, terrain and 3D Tiles used by the demo and examples.

## Company sponsorship and support

If your team needs custom development, deployment guidance, prioritized features or consulting, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Yoy0o/TerraView/issues/new/choose) briefly describing what you need, and the maintainer will get back to you about how to continue the conversation. Company sponsors can have their logo shown in the README and on the documentation site.

## Other ways to help

- **Star** the repository on GitHub so more people can find it.
- Use it in your project and [report back](https://github.com/Yoy0o/TerraView/issues) problems, performance numbers and ideas.
- Contribute code or documentation (see the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Yoy0o/TerraView/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.en.md)).
- Mention TerraView in your articles and talks.
